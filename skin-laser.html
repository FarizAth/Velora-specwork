Clinical Technologies

Skin & Energy Treatments

RF Microneedling

Combines physical micro-channels with targeted thermal energy to stimulate structural elastin and collagen in deeper dermal layers.

Ideal for: Laxity, texture, scarring

IPL Photofacial

Broad-spectrum light pulses targeting hemoglobin and melanin to diminish diffuse redness, broken capillaries, and sun discoloration.

Ideal for: Pigment, sun spots, rosacea

Laser Resurfacing

Precision fractional light energy removing damaged surface layers while activating fresh tissue regeneration underneath.

Ideal for: Deep lines, tone, rebuild

Medical Chemical Peels

Formulated acid solutions adjusted to your specific skin tolerance to shed dull surface skin and encourage cell turnover.

Ideal for: Clarity, dullness, acne congestion

Advanced Microneedling

Micro-channeling therapy paired with restorative growth serums to encourage skin repair and improve overall skin density.

Ideal for: Pore size, fine lines

Hyperpigmentation Protocol

Targeted combined therapy incorporating specialized chemical agents and light therapy to inhibit dark pigment synthesis.

Ideal for: Melasma, post-acne marks
